Biography

I’m a research assistant at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology, Kanpur India. I have been fortunate to have got the opportunity to work under the supervision of Prof. Piyush Rai. We are currently working on some exciting problems at the intersection of deep Learning and probabilistic machine learning. Prior to that I worked on applications of deep Learning and sensor fusion in autonomous vehicles under Prof. Gaurav Pandey.

I am especially interested in Neural Networks and their applications in Vision and Language.

Please feel free to contact me at nikhilmehta.dce@gmail.com.
